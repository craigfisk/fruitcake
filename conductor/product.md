# Product Guide

## Initial Concept
An app to send fruitcake notes by email and see where they go on a Google Map

## Goals
The primary goal of the Fruitcake App is to provide a fun and unique way for users to send digital greetings. Subscribers can select a photo of a fruitcake and email it to others. Recipients can then forward the fruitcake photo to more people, and each person along the way can view where the fruitcake has been, using Google Maps.

## Target Audience
The app is for people looking for a fun and unique way to send digital greetings.

## Key Features
- A gallery of high-quality fruitcake images for selection.
- Integration with email services for sending notes.
- A map or tracking feature to visualize the photo's journey.
