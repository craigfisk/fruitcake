# Product Guidelines

## Overall Tone and Style
The desired overall tone and style for the Fruitcake App is playful and whimsical. This approach will use a lighthearted and fun aesthetic, incorporating bright colors and quirky imagery to enhance the festive fruitcake theme.

## Brand Messaging
The brand messaging will be innovative and tech-savvy, focusing on the unique digital aspects of the app. This will showcase a modern approach to sharing and tracking.

## Visual Identity
The visual identity of the app will be bright and colorful. It will utilize a vibrant color palette with lively illustrations and playful typography to create an energetic and engaging feel.
